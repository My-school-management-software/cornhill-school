# CORNHILL Microsoft Store PWA release pack

**Status:** preparation only — not submitted to Microsoft Store.  
**Target:** the single central CORNHILL application at `https://cornhill-school-swart.vercel.app/`.  
**Distribution choice:** Microsoft Store PWA package generated with PWABuilder.  
**Intended platforms:** Windows 10 and Windows 11.

## Why this route

CORNHILL is primarily a hosted HTML/CSS/JavaScript PWA. Microsoft's current guidance recommends the PWA route for web apps: reserve an app name in Partner Center, package the HTTPS PWA with PWABuilder, and submit the generated Windows packages. Microsoft signs the Store package and manages package distribution. Normal website-code and service-worker updates are delivered by the hosted PWA at next launch; changes to the web manifest require a new Store package submission.

This is different from the native Tauri EXE build. The existing unsigned Tauri EXE is a test artifact only and is not the Microsoft Store release package.

## School-owned account and publisher identity

A school Director or other authorised adult representative must own and complete the Microsoft Partner Center registration and identity/business verification. Do not use a student's identity or submit a student's ID/selfie for the school's publishing account. Choose the account type that correctly reflects the school's legal status. Microsoft currently offers a no-registration-fee onboarding route for individual and company developers, but verification and Store certification are still required.

Start at: https://storedeveloper.microsoft.com/

The authorised adult must:

1. Sign in using the school's approved Microsoft account.
2. Select the correct publisher account type for the school.
3. Complete Microsoft's identity or organisation verification personally.
4. Open Partner Center → Apps and games → New product → MSIX or PWA app.
5. Reserve the preferred name **CORNHILL School Management System** (availability is not guaranteed).
6. Open Product management → Product identity and record:
   - Package ID
   - Publisher ID
   - Publisher display name

Those three values are package identity metadata. They are not passwords, but should still be checked carefully and copied exactly. Do not invent these identifiers or use placeholder publisher details for a production submission.

## Package with PWABuilder

1. Open https://www.pwabuilder.com/
2. Enter `https://cornhill-school-swart.vercel.app/` and start the PWA assessment.
3. Review the report card and resolve all blocking action items.
4. Choose **Package For Stores** → **Windows** → **Generate Package**.
5. Enter the exact Package ID, Publisher ID and Publisher display name copied from the approved Partner Center reservation.
6. Generate and download the ZIP. The Microsoft PWA publishing guide describes a package set containing a modern `.msixbundle` and a classic `.appxbundle` for Windows-version coverage.
7. Keep the packages together and do not edit or repackage their contents after generation.

The required Publisher identity values are not known yet, so a production-identifiable package cannot be generated by this repository alone.

## Store submission checklist

The authorised adult publisher completes the Store submission in Partner Center:

- [ ] Product reservation and publisher identity verified.
- [ ] PWABuilder report card reviewed; blocking items resolved.
- [ ] Modern `.msixbundle` uploaded.
- [ ] Classic `.appxbundle` uploaded.
- [ ] App name, description, category and age-rating questionnaire completed accurately.
- [ ] Pricing set to Free if the school intends the app to be free to install.
- [ ] Distribution markets selected by the school (Uganda should be explicitly considered).
- [ ] Privacy policy URL published and approved by the school.
- [ ] Store logo and at least one real app screenshot supplied; use test/demo records only, never real learner, guardian or staff personal information.
- [ ] Website and support contact details supplied by the school.
- [ ] Certification notes explain that access to school records requires an authorised school account; public visitors must not be shown private school records.
- [ ] Submit for Microsoft certification and wait for the actual Partner Center result.

## Privacy policy publication blocker

CORNHILL processes personal information relating to learners, guardians and staff and may handle academic, attendance, finance and uploaded school-document records. A real, school-approved privacy policy is required before Store submission. Microsoft may require a privacy URL when an app can access, collect or transmit personal information.

The school must approve and publish a policy that accurately names:
- the school/controller and an official contact channel for privacy requests;
- the categories of learner, guardian, staff and account information processed;
- why each category is collected and who is authorised to access it;
- cloud hosting/database and any configured messaging or other service providers;
- how uploads and records are stored, retained, corrected and deleted;
- the process for access, correction and privacy complaints;
- the applicable school/legal retention schedule and incident contact.

Do not publish a generic placeholder as the final privacy policy. The school should obtain appropriate privacy/legal review before exposing a public privacy URL. No legal compliance certification is made by this technical release pack.

- [ ] Obtain written school approval before submission.

## Update policy after Store approval

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript and service-worker updates to the hosted website normally reach the installed PWA when the app is launched/reloaded.
- Changes to the web manifest, package identity, publisher identity, app icon or package metadata require a new package and Store submission.
- Microsoft Store package updates go through Store certification and then are delivered by the Store.
- Keep the production URL and origin stable. Do not move the app to a different origin without planning a migration.
