## 2. Information the system may process

Depending on the modules enabled by the school, the system may process:

- **Learner information:** names, admission identifiers, Ministry LIN and examination identifiers, class/stream, learner status, academic marks, report information, attendance, and profile photographs.
- **Parent and guardian information:** names, relationship to learner, phone numbers, approved contact details, portal-access and verification information.
- **Staff information:** names, work contact details, job roles, account and permission information, attendance and school employment records.
- **School finance information:** school charges, bursary arrangements, payment amounts, allocation and balance information, receipt references and payment-related contact details.
- **School documents and evidence:** files and photographs submitted to support school workflows, including administrative and financial evidence.
- **Technical/account information:** sign-in and session information, role assignments, security/audit events, and safe local form drafts where the offline feature is used.

The school must remove any category that is not actually collected and add any category used by a deployed module but missing from this list.

## 3. Why information is processed

The school uses information to administer admissions and enrolment, identify learners, manage teaching and assessment, prepare reports, manage attendance, administer school fees and receipts, communicate school-related information, protect school records, and provide controlled access to authorised users and approved guardians.

The system must not be used to create unrelated profiles, advertise to learners, or expose one learner's information to another family.

## 4. Who can access information

Access is intended to be limited by authenticated accounts, roles and the school-approved permissions attached to those accounts. Guardian access is intended to be separated from the administration workspace and limited to the learner(s) linked to that approved guardian.

The school must verify user permissions, guardian-to-learner links, and administrative access before relying on these controls for live records. Do not assume a feature is safe solely because it appears in the interface.

## 5. Hosting and service providers

The current application architecture uses Vercel to host the web application and Supabase services for application data, authentication and private file storage. The school should review the provider terms, processing locations, security settings and any applicable data-processing terms before approving this policy.

SMS, WhatsApp, fingerprint-device vendors, printers or other external services must only be named as data recipients after the school has configured and approved those integrations. If an external provider receives personal information, the policy must identify the provider and explain the purpose and data involved.

## 6. Local/offline use

The web application can cache public application resources and save selected safe form drafts locally on a device. Private database records and server-backed actions are not made available offline by this feature. Local drafts may remain on the device until submitted or removed; users should use school-managed devices, protect device access, and avoid leaving an account open on a shared computer.

The school should document how shared devices are secured and how staff clear locally stored drafts when a device is reassigned or retired.

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

The school should disclose information only to authorised persons and service providers who need it to carry out the school's stated purposes, or where disclosure is otherwise required or permitted by applicable law. The school must review and list any routine disclosures, integrations and service providers before publishing this policy.

## 8. Retention and deletion

The school is responsible for setting and applying a retention schedule for learner, academic, finance, staff and evidence records in accordance with applicable requirements and legitimate school recordkeeping needs. Do not insert an arbitrary number of days in this policy. The school must complete the actual retention periods and deletion/archiving procedure before approval.

## 9. Requests, corrections and complaints

A learner's parent/guardian or a staff member should contact the school's confirmed privacy contact to request access to, correction of, or review of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. The school must verify the requester's authority before disclosing or changing records.

**Privacy contact:** [school to complete before publication]

## 10. Security

The system is designed to use HTTPS, authenticated access, role-based permissions and database access policies. The school remains responsible for issuing and revoking accounts, choosing strong credentials, protecting devices, reviewing access, and promptly reporting suspected loss or unauthorised disclosure. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

The school should validate its live configuration, backup and recovery process, account protection settings, and device procedures before this section is approved.

## 11. Changes to this policy

The school should update this policy when its data uses, modules, service providers, retention practices or legal obligations change. The published copy must show its effective date and version.
