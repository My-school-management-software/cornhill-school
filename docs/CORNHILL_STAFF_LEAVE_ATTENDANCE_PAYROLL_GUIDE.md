# CORNHILL Staff Operations — Leave, Attendance Review & Payroll Payment Register

The local staff database supports leave requests with independent review, date-overlap protection and an append-only leave balance ledger. Leave usage must refer to an approved request and cannot make a balance negative.

Daily attendance reviews are separate append-only events tied to the original staff member and work date. A staff member cannot review their own attendance.

Payroll disbursements are recorded only after payroll is posted. The register supports Cash, Bank Transfer and Mobile Money evidence, partial payments, independent verification and a remaining-net-pay summary. It does not initiate a transfer or automatically deduct salary.

The permission catalogue includes separate leave management, leave review, leave-balance management, attendance review, payroll disbursement recording and payroll disbursement verification permissions. Role grants remain empty pending an approved school permission matrix.
