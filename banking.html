Register a school bank account

Only the two school-approved institutions are available. Enter the exact account-holder name, last four account digits and verified opening balance from the bank record. Full account numbers and passwords must not be stored here.

Record a bank deposit or withdrawal request

A deposit automatically requests a bank-slip photo from the selected authorised phone. A withdrawal is recorded as a request and cannot affect the verified balance until an administrator approves it and its evidence is reviewed.

Do not record the same deposit twice. Check the bank reference and the date before submitting. A photograph is supporting evidence; it is not automatic approval.
The request is sent to the staff member assigned to this phone. The phone must be active and online to receive an immediate notification.

Submit a small school expense for approval

The Deputy Bursar can submit expenses within limits set by an administrator. This is a request, not a posted expense. Do not pay the supplier until the request is approved. After payment, the authorised phone must photograph the receipt or invoice.

How verification works

The phone user submits one or more clear photographs or PDFs. An administrator reviews the evidence separately in the Evidence Capture register. Only an administrator's recorded approval changes a deposit or withdrawal to Verified. Rejected evidence can be requested again.
