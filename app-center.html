CORNHILL Attendance Terminal

A separate installable attendance app for a designated Android phone, tablet or office device. It is designed to receive the method selected by the Director and send verified attendance events to the Main System.

Methods planned: QR staff badge, NFC card/tag, supported phone biometrics, fingerprint/face attendance machines and approved vendor adapters.

Current build: installable interface shell. Device pairing and live event submission are not enabled until the secure Main System connection is completed and tested.
