Parent Admissions Account

Create your own email-and-password account to submit an admission enquiry and return later to check its progress. Your account can see only enquiries submitted from that account. It does not provide access to school administration records. On Windows, open this page in Chrome or Edge and choose Install CORNHILL Parent Admissions from the browser menu. On Android, use the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.

Do not upload identity documents, birth certificates, medical details or payment evidence to this public page. Once the school begins formal admission, the office will request documents through its private evidence process.
