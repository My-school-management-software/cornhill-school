ADMISSIONS & ENQUIRIES

Enquire about admission

Complete this short form and the school administration can contact you about admission steps. This is an enquiry only; it does not register the learner or guarantee a place.

For privacy, do not submit birth certificates, identity documents, medical information, photographs or payment details through this public form. Formal documents are handled later through the school's approved process.

After you submit, the school must review the enquiry and contact you. For an approved application, formal registration and learner-code creation are completed by the school office.
